## Why Consult a Neurologist?

Neurologists are medical specialists trained to diagnose and treat disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves, and muscles. They manage both acute and chronic neurological conditions using advanced diagnostic techniques and evidence-based treatments.

You should consider visiting a neurologist if you experience:

* Frequent or severe headaches
* Migraine attacks
* Epilepsy or seizures
* Stroke symptoms
* Parkinson's disease
* Memory loss or dementia
* Multiple sclerosis
* Neuropathy
* Muscle weakness
* Balance or coordination problems
* Numbness or tingling sensations
* Sleep-related neurological disorders

Early diagnosis often leads to better treatment outcomes and helps prevent disease progression.
